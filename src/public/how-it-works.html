How it works

An on-chain audit, not a guess.

ReviewBot.Celo turns live repository evidence and real Celo on-chain wallet activity into a structured trust verdict, instead of relying on generic scoring prompts.

Usefulness

Checks whether the project can do what it claims and whether the claims are observable.

Safety

Looks for dangerous execution paths, trust gaps, weak validation, and operational risk.

Economic viability

Assesses whether the system has a believable value and cost profile rather than hand-wavy economics.

Celo integration

Checks whether Celo is real product infrastructure rather than a superficial mention.

Engineering maturity

Evaluates maintainability signals such as docs, tests, CI, and implementation hygiene.
